1. Plain-Language Overview: What Was Tested & Why
Long-Horizon Agent Endurance

Most academic benchmarks evaluate models over short 8K or 32K context windows. In production, however, autonomous agents (such as software refactoring pipelines, devops bots, and deep research agents) run continuously for hundreds or thousands of steps, accumulating massive multi-megatoken execution traces.

Ultra Bench pushes this to the absolute extreme:

  • 3,000 Continuous Steps & 2,025,408 Tokens: Simulating weeks of tool operations with burst sizes up to 8,192 tokens/turn.
  • 10 Invariant Needles & Adversarial Near-Miss Decoys: Needles are planted across depths from 3.3% to 96.7%, surrounded by adversarial decoys ($\cos heta = 0.88 - 0.94$) designed to trick attention into retrieving fake instructions.
  • Monolithic Failure: Uncompressed attention crashes at Step 330 on 48GB Apple Silicon hardware. By Step 3,000, uncompressed attention would require an impossible 432.7 GB of VRAM.

StrataKV's Architectural Triumph (100% Untrained): Survived the full 3,000 steps with 10/10 needles retrieved at Rank 1, holding memory to 1.11 GB (Pure) and 265 MB (with Elle Conductor) with zero model weight modification.
